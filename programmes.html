Your trading journey starts with understanding

From market curiosity
to a clearer process.

At first, charts, unfamiliar terms and fast price moves can feel like noise. The first step is not to chase a trade. It is to learn what you are looking at, understand the risk, and ask better questions. Markets Wealth offers beginner education, one-to-one mentorship and community options to support different stages of that journey. No service can remove market risk or promise an outcome.

Learn at your own pace

A path from learning to informed decisions.

The goal is not to predict every move. It is to build understanding, consider your risk and decide for yourself.

  1. 01

    Start with the language of the market.

    Learn chart reading, order types and risk management through the Beginners programme. Build a foundation before deciding how you want to participate.

  2. 02

    Work through your own process.

    One-to-one mentorship offers focused guidance with Khanya. Bring your questions and discuss the approach you want to develop; confirm the session format and inclusions first.

  3. 03

    Choose support without giving up judgment.

    Signals and Discord conversations can provide market ideas and community discussion. They are not instructions or guarantees: you choose whether to act and remain responsible for your risk.
