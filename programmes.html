Your trading journey starts with understanding

From market curiosity
to a clearer process.

I began trading in high school and have spent 7–8 years learning through real market conditions. At MarketWealth, we teach the thinking behind a trade: read the context, wait for a setup, plan what would prove the idea wrong, then review the decision. The goal is to understand why a trade is being considered—not just see an entry.

Learn at your own pace

A repeatable way to study a trade idea.

We move from context to setup, risk and review. This gives you a clear way to question an idea before deciding what to do.

  1. 01

    Read the context.

    Ask whether price is trending or ranging, mark important reaction areas, and consider the session or news context before focusing on an entry.

  2. 02

    Wait for a setup.

    Decide what price action would support your idea and what would cancel it. If the conditions are missing, patience is part of the plan.

  3. 03

    Plan the risk.

    Know where the idea is invalidated and decide your stop and position size before entry. The possible loss belongs in the plan, not as an afterthought.

  4. 04

    Review the trade.

    Record the reason, execution and outcome. A winning trade can break the plan; a losing trade can follow it. Review helps separate process from luck.
