THE MARKETS, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Know what you're
getting into.

New to trading? Start by understanding the service you are considering. Ask the team what each programme includes before you pay.

01 / FOREX

What is forex?

Forex is the exchange of one currency for another. Prices move as buyers and sellers respond to economic news, events and market activity.

02 / SYNTHETICS

What are synthetic markets?

Synthetic indices are broker-provided, simulated markets. They are different from currency pairs and still involve significant trading risk.

03 / SIGNALS

What is a signal?

A signal is a market idea shared for consideration, not a promise or a requirement to trade. Confirm what information and support are included with the team.

04 / COPY TRADING

What is copy trading?

Copy trading attempts to mirror another account's trades. It does not remove risk: copied trades can lose money, and your results may differ.

! These are general explanations, not trading instructions or financial advice.

How do I join a programme?

Choose an option and message the team on WhatsApp. Confirm availability, what is included, and any terms before paying. Once confirmed, follow the payment instructions and send your proof of payment.

Which option is right for a beginner?

The Beginners programme is listed as the beginner-focused option. Message the team to confirm its curriculum, format, duration and support before enrolling.

What is the difference between the group and one-on-one options?

The Signals Cartel Group is listed as a group signal option. One on one is a private mentorship option. Ask the team to explain the specific sessions, access and support included in each.

What happens in the Discord trading sessions?

Discord is the community space for streamed trading sessions and live market discussion. Ask the team about the session schedule, how to access streams, and whether replays are available. Streams are not a guarantee of results or personal financial advice.

What will I find on YouTube?

Khanya's YouTube is for vlogs and getting to know the mentor and Markets Wealth. Watch the featured day-in-the-life vlog or visit the channel for more content and programme promotion.

What is the difference between forex and synthetic signals?

Forex concerns currency markets. Synthetic indices are simulated markets offered by brokers. The Synthetics Signals option is listed separately; confirm exactly which markets and access it covers before joining.

What does the monthly or lifetime group price mean?

The listed Signals Cartel Group options are R1,000 monthly or R3,000 lifetime. Confirm what “lifetime” covers, how access is delivered, and any applicable terms with the team before paying.

What does the programme price include? Is there a refund policy?

Only the programme names and prices are listed here. Ask the team to confirm the full inclusions, duration, access arrangements, and refund or cancellation terms before making payment.

How do I pay?

Pay to the Tyme Bank account shown on this site using an immediate payment. Use your name and surname as the reference, then send your proof of payment to the team.

When will I get copy-trader details?

An admin will share copy-trader details when they get back to you. No specific response time is provided, so please contact the team if you need an update.

Can I lose money using signals or copy trading?

No. Trading carries substantial risk and results are never guaranteed. You can lose some or all of your money. Review the risk disclaimer on this page and make your own informed decisions.
