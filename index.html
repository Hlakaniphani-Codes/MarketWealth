The process I teach

A trade idea needs more than an entry.

We start with the market around the setup, decide what would confirm or invalidate the idea, and review the decision after the trade. That is how experience becomes a process.

  1. 01 / CONTEXT

    Read the market first.

    Is price trending or ranging? Where has it reacted before, and what could move the market today?

  2. 02 / SETUP

    Wait for a reason to act.

    Define what you need to see before entering. If the setup is not there, waiting is a decision too.

  3. 03 / RISK

    Know your invalidation.

    Plan the stop and position size before entry, so the possible loss is considered before the trade begins.

  4. 04 / REVIEW

    Learn from the decision.

    Record the context, plan and result. A good process matters more than judging one trade by its outcome.
