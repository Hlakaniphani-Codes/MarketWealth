Questions

Before you begin.

A few honest answers to help you decide with confidence.

Can beginners join?+

Yes. The Beginners programme is intended for people who need structure and a foundation in chart reading, risk management and order types. It is education, not a shortcut.

Do signals guarantee profit?+

No. No signal, strategy or mentor can guarantee results. Trading involves risk, and you can lose some or all of your capital.

How do I choose the right option?+

Message the team with your experience, goals and preferred level of support. We can explain the difference before you make a payment.

How do payments work?+

Confirm the programme and payment details with the team first, then send proof of payment on WhatsApp. Access follows only after confirmation.
