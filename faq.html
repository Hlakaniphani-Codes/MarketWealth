Questions

Before you begin.

A few honest answers to help you decide with confidence.

Can beginners join?+

Yes. We start with the language of the chart: candles, trend and range, key reaction areas, order types and how to plan risk. You build understanding step by step before deciding how you want to trade.

What do we look at before a trade idea?+

I start with market context: is price trending or ranging, where has it reacted, and what is happening around the session? Then we look for a setup, decide what would confirm or invalidate it, and plan risk before entry. Not every chart has a trade worth taking.

Do signals guarantee profit?+

No one can promise a result from a signal or strategy. We share market ideas and the thinking behind them; you decide whether they fit your own plan. Markets can move against any setup.

When do live trading sessions take place?+

Our live trading is event-focused, around major scheduled releases such as CPI (the inflation report), NFP (the US nonfarm payrolls report) and FOMC (Federal Reserve policy announcements). In Discord, we discuss the market context and our decisions as the event unfolds. Ask us about upcoming sessions and access.

Can I learn if I work a 9-to-5?+

Many people balance trading with work and other responsibilities. Tell us how much time you can give the charts; we’ll explain the learning and support options so you can choose a routine that fits your life.

What is the difference between signals and copy trading?+

With signals, you review an idea and decide whether to act. Copy trading can place trades in your account through a connected setup. Ask our team how the copy option works, including sizing, access and terms, before you decide.

How do I choose the right option?+

Tell us your experience, goals, schedule and the kind of guidance you prefer. We’ll explain the differences between beginner education, one-to-one mentorship, signals and copy trading before you make a payment.

Which broker do you use?+

I trade with and recommend Space Markets. Our signup link includes a partner code; see the Space Markets link and review the broker’s terms before opening an account.

How do payments work?+

We’ll confirm the programme, price and payment details first. Once you choose to proceed, send proof of payment on WhatsApp and we’ll follow up about access.
