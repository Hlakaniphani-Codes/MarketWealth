About Khanya Dlodlongwana

A trader's journey.
A mentor's purpose.

Real market experience, shared with the next generation of traders.

Khanya began trading while still in high school. Over the past 7–8 years, he has built his skill through hands-on experience, learning to read the market and make decisions under pressure.

His own journey has included growing trading accounts through real market practice. Now he teaches the thinking behind his approach: how to read charts, understand a trade idea, plan a decision and build discipline over time.

Markets Wealth brings that experience to traders at different stages—from people learning the foundations to those looking for direct mentorship or a community to learn alongside. The aim is to help each person understand the process, not follow blindly.

Khanya believes in real trading, practical education and sharing the skill he has spent years developing.
