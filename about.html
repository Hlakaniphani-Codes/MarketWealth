Our story

My trading journey.
Our purpose as a team.

I share what I have learned; we help traders build their own process.

I started trading while I was still in high school. For 7–8 years, day trading has challenged me to keep learning as price, volatility and market conditions change. I have personally flipped accounts, but the lasting lesson has been that skill comes from patience, practice and discipline—not one lucky entry.

When I study a trade, I begin with context: is price trending or ranging, where has it reacted, and what would confirm or invalidate my idea? I plan risk before entry and review the decision afterwards. That is the thinking I bring to MarketWealth.

Our team works with traders at different stages. We provide structured learning, focused mentorship and event-based live trading around releases such as CPI, NFP and FOMC. In each setting, we explain the market context and decisions so you can build a process of your own.

I believe in real trading and honest learning. We are here to share experience, explain our approach and help you find support that fits your goals and routine.

Khanya

Founder & Mentor, MarketWealth
