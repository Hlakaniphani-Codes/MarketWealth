Getting started

Let’s find your starting point.

Tell us what you already know, how much time you can give the charts and what you want to learn next. We’ll walk you through the options so you can choose with clarity—not pressure.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your routine.

    Are you new to forex, already trading, focused on synthetics, or balancing the market with a 9-to-5? We’ll start from where you are.

  2. 02

    Explore your options.

    We’ll explain the differences between beginner education, one-to-one mentorship, group signals and copy trading.

  3. 03

    Ask what is included.

    Confirm the price, access, schedule, support format and terms before you decide. We want you to know what you are joining.

  4. 04

    Build a steady routine.

    Study market context, wait for a setup, plan your risk before entry and review your decisions as you learn.

A simple way to think through a trade

Before an entry, ask: What is price doing? What would confirm my idea? Where is it invalidated? How much could I lose if I am wrong? If those answers are not clear, pause and keep learning.
